# South Florida PE Firms Tracker

Maintained by Rex (Research Analyst). See `agents/rex.md` — Standing Responsibilities — PE Firm Research.

**Scope:** PE firms headquartered in or actively investing in South Florida (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach), lower-middle-market/SME focus (~$5M–$75M EBITDA portfolio companies). Excludes VC, growth equity, and mega-funds (>$1B AUM).

**Sourcing key:** ✅ Confirmed = firm's own website, an SEC/state filing, or a direct press release (BusinessWire, PRNewswire, PRWeb, or the firm's own newsroom). Est. = third-party aggregator (PitchBook, Crunchbase, ZoomInfo, Tracxn, Preqin, RocketReach, CB Insights, etc.) or trade press reporting a figure without a cited primary source — not to be treated as fact until verified.

**Last refreshed:** 2026-08-12 (initial seed, based on research session same day)

1. **Blue Sea Capital** (West Palm Beach) — confirmed, named Operating Partner (Harris D. Thompson) *and* an explicit self-described tech-modernization value-creation platform (cloud migration, data analytics, CRM/ERP). Follow-up research (2026-08-12) found this is delivered through **outside vendors engaged per portfolio company, not an in-house digital/technology function** — the firm's own site solicits vendors for "digital transformation, ERP and data-enabled decision making," and no Head of Digital/Technology/Data role exists anywhere in the team roster; Thompson's own background is operations/healthcare, not technology. That's a *stronger* fit for North Star, not a weaker one — it means the gap Tonya would fill is real, not already covered internally. AUM is still only an estimate (~$1.5B), which would put it over the $1B screen if confirmed — worth a direct question early in outreach.
2. **Firelight Capital Partners** (Fort Lauderdale) — digital transformation is a named value-creation lever on the firm's own site, but no individual contact is confirmed yet. High priority for the contact-research pass below.
3. **Alterna Equity Partners** (Fort Lauderdale) — confirmed contact (Rob Konrad, Managing Partner) and an explicit Technology investment vertical; portfolio company Mod Op is self-described as AI/data-science-driven.
4. **MBF Healthcare Partners** (Coral Gables) — confirmed contact and a fresh, press-confirmed acquisition platform (Arete Health, Sept 2025) in a sector with clear AI ROI (healthcare services).
5. **New Water Capital** (Boca Raton) — confirmed Operating Partner (Dwight Closson) via the firm's own press release, and a confirmed $406M Fund I — one of the most fully-verified profiles in this set.
