I used to think I was late.

Everyone around me seemed to already understand what I was still trying to figure out — what AI actually meant for my work, my relevance, my next chapter.

Then I realized: most of them were performing confidence, not living it.

Here's what I've come to believe. The women who will lead in this next era aren't the ones who learned every tool. They're the ones who trusted that their decades of judgment — reading rooms, navigating complexity, knowing when something is off — is exactly what this moment needs more of, not less.

AI can move fast. Wisdom knows which direction to point it.

You are not behind. You are seasoned. That is not the same thing.
