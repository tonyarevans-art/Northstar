## THE BIG IDEA

Most professionals spend years building deep expertise — then wonder why they're
still being overlooked, over-explained, or underutilized. The problem isn't the
expertise. It's that expertise alone has never been enough to build authority.
Authority is built differently. And it can be learned.

---

## SIGNATURE KEYNOTE

**From Expertise to Authority: How Leaders Build Influence That Opens Doors**

In a world that rewards visibility as much as competence, the leaders who advance
are not always the most qualified — they're the ones who learned to translate what
they know into recognized authority.

This keynote introduces The Authority Economy, a three-part framework built for
leaders who are ready to stop waiting to be discovered:

- **Clarity** — Get precise about the specific value you bring and who needs it most.
  Vague expertise is invisible expertise.
- **Distinction** — Define what makes your point of view, your methodology, and your
  presence unmistakably yours. Not a brand. A position.
- **Expression** — Build the habit of showing up — in rooms, in writing, in
  conversation — in ways that make your authority legible to others.

Available in 30-, 45-, or 60-minute formats. Adaptable for keynote, breakout,
and executive leadership sessions.

**Audience outcomes:**
- A framework for turning deep expertise into recognized authority
- Language to describe their own value without over-explaining it
- A clear next move to build visibility inside or beyond their organization
