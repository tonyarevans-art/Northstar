Most leaders aren't behind on AI because they lack access.

They're behind because no one handed them a decision framework — just a list of tools.

Here's what I see across the organizations I work with: the leaders who are getting traction with AI aren't the ones who adopted it fastest. They're the ones who got clear on what they were actually trying to change before they touched a single platform.

AI doesn't fix a fuzzy strategy. It amplifies whatever clarity — or confusion — already exists.

The move isn't "which tool should we pilot." The move is: what decision do we want to make faster, cheaper, or at greater scale? Start there. The tool selection becomes obvious.

Leaders who frame it that way stop chasing and start building.
