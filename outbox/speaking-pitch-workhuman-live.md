**Proposed session title:** From Expertise to Authority: What Actually Determines Whether AI Adoption Sticks

**Session concept (for the proposal form):**

Workplace culture and HR audiences are being told AI adoption is a technology rollout. It isn't — it's a trust and change-leadership problem wearing a technology costume. This session breaks down why so many AI initiatives stall after the tools are already purchased and live, using the Authority Economy framework (Clarity, Distinction, Expression) to give HR and culture leaders a concrete way to lead adoption instead of reacting to it.

**Why WorkHuman's audience specifically:** WorkHuman's community already treats "how people experience work" as a core discipline, not an afterthought — which makes this the right room for a session that argues AI adoption is fundamentally a people-experience problem, not an IT one.
