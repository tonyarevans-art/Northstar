**Proposed session title:** From Expertise to Authority: Leading AI Adoption Through Change, Not Around It

**Abstract (150–200 words):**

Most AI rollouts fail for the same reason most change initiatives fail: the technology gets selected correctly and the organization still doesn't adopt it, because nobody planned for how people would need to change alongside it. This session applies change-management fundamentals — the discipline talent development professionals already know — to the specific, high-stakes case of AI adoption inside organizations.

Drawing on the Authority Economy framework (Clarity, Distinction, Expression), this session walks through how to diagnose readiness before a rollout starts, where AI initiatives actually break down in practice, and how L&D and talent development leaders can position themselves as the function that makes adoption stick — not the function brought in after the technology has already failed to take.

Attendees will leave with a practical readiness-assessment framework they can apply to their own organization's next AI or digital transformation initiative.

**Why this fits ATD South Florida's audience:** Talent development professionals are already the internal owners of "how people learn and change" — this session reframes AI adoption as their problem to lead, not IT's problem to solve alone.
